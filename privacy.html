Privacy
policy

Your information. Our responsibility.

The Youth Action Conference (YAC) respects your privacy. We collect only the information we reasonably need to organize the conference, communicate with participants, provide appropriate support, and keep our online community safe.

YAC is an independent youth initiative. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Full privacy policy document

The live policy is maintained in Google Docs. You can also download a PDF copy for your records or to share with a parent, guardian, or school.

View in Google Docs ↗ Download PDF ↓

1. Information we collect

When you register for or participate in YAC, we may collect information you choose to provide, including:

  • Your name and preferred name
  • Email address
  • Age range
  • Country or region
  • City
  • School, university, organization, or affiliation
  • Participant type
  • Topics and activities that interest you
  • Responses about your interests, perspectives, and conference goals
  • Accessibility, language, communication, technology, or participation support you request
  • Your communication preferences
  • How you heard about YAC
  • Whether you would like to hear about future YAC events

We may also collect information you provide when contacting the YAC team or participating in conference activities. We aim to collect only information that has a clear purpose.

2. Accessibility & support information

You may choose to tell us about adjustments or support that would help you participate.

You do not need to provide a diagnosis or detailed medical information when requesting support.

Accessibility information will be used only as reasonably necessary to plan and provide appropriate participation support and should only be accessible to organizers or facilitators who need that information.

3. Why we use your information

We may use your information to:

  • Process your conference registration
  • Confirm your participation
  • Send joining links and conference information
  • Provide schedules and program updates
  • Organize sessions and activities
  • Understand participant interests
  • Provide requested accessibility or participation support
  • Create participation certificates
  • Respond to questions or support requests
  • Help maintain a safe conference environment
  • Evaluate and improve YAC
  • Contact you about future YAC activities if you have chosen to receive those communications

We will not use accessibility information for marketing.

4. Young participants

YAC is a youth-centered event and some participants may be under 18. We therefore aim to collect as little personal information about young participants as reasonably necessary.

Where parental or guardian permission is required for a particular activity, recording, or use of personal information, YAC may request appropriate consent separately.

Participants should avoid sharing private information such as their home address, personal phone number, passwords, or other unnecessary personal information during public conference sessions.

5. Photos, screenshots & recordings

Some YAC sessions or activities may be photographed, screenshotted, or recorded. Participants will be informed when an official recording is taking place.

Where registration provides a choice about public media use, we will respect that choice.

If you have not agreed to public use of your image or name, YAC should take reasonable steps to exclude you from publicly shared identifiable conference media.

Participants must not independently record, photograph, or screenshot other participants in ways prohibited by the YAC Community Guidelines.

6. Who can access your information

Participant information may be accessible to members of the YAC organizing team who need it to organize or support the conference.

Limited information may also need to be shared with moderators, facilitators, or other people supporting the event when necessary — for example, to provide an accessibility adjustment.

We will not sell participant personal information.

We will not provide participant contact lists to sponsors, speakers, partner organizations, or other participants for their own marketing purposes.

7. Services we use

YAC may use third-party services to operate the conference, such as:

  • Google Forms or similar registration tools
  • Google Workspace
  • Zoom or Google Meet
  • Email services
  • Website hosting and related web services

These services may process technical or personal information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Participants should be aware that online conference platforms may display information such as their chosen display name, profile image, camera feed, microphone audio, chat contributions, or reactions to other people in a session.

8. Website information

When you visit the YAC website, the website or its hosting provider may automatically process basic technical information needed to deliver and secure the site.

If YAC introduces analytics, cookies, tracking technologies, or other data collection beyond what is technically necessary, this Privacy Policy should be updated to explain what is being collected and why.

We do not intend to use unnecessary tracking simply because it is available.

9. How long we keep information

We should keep personal information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected.

Registration information may be retained for a limited period after the conference so that YAC can:

  • Provide certificates
  • Respond to participant questions
  • Evaluate the event
  • Manage safeguarding or conduct matters where necessary
  • Meet any applicable administrative or legal obligations

Information that is no longer needed should be deleted or anonymized where reasonably possible.

If you separately choose to receive information about future YAC events, the contact information needed for that purpose may be retained until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove it.

10. Your choices

Depending on the circumstances and applicable privacy law, you may be able to ask YAC to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Update your registration information
  • Withdraw an optional consent
  • Stop sending you optional communications
  • Delete personal information that is no longer necessary
  • Discuss how your information has been used

Some information may need to be retained when there is a legitimate safeguarding, security, administrative, or legal reason to do so.

11. Protecting your information

YAC will take reasonable steps to protect participant information and limit access to people who need it.

However, no online system can guarantee absolute security. Participants should also protect their own privacy by avoiding unnecessary sharing of personal information during conference sessions.

12. Safeguarding & safety

There may be exceptional circumstances in which information needs to be used or shared to address a serious safety, safeguarding, legal, or security concern.

In such circumstances, YAC should share only the information reasonably necessary with appropriate people or authorities.

Protecting a young person's safety may sometimes take priority over keeping information confidential.

13. Future communications

Registering for YAC does not automatically mean that you agree to receive marketing or announcements about future events.

Where offered, participants can choose separately whether they would like to receive future YAC communications. You can change that choice later.

14. Changes to this policy

YAC may update this Privacy Policy as the conference develops, particularly if new platforms, registration processes, recording practices, or services are introduced.

The latest version is available on this page and in our Google Doc.

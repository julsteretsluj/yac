02

The
conference

A two-day virtual summit where young people lead the conversation on human rights.

Youth Action Conference brings together student leaders, youth advocates, and changemakers from every continent for masterclasses, workshops, and open debate.

Each edition spans two days with four timed sessions — so participants in Asia, Africa, the Americas, and the Pacific each have a live window to join. Organized by IOY Ambassadors, YAC is peer-led and action-oriented. Participants leave with certificates, new connections, and concrete next steps for projects they can launch in their schools and communities.

04

What
happens

Six hours of structured programming in every session block.

  1. 01

    Masterclass

    Keynotes from journalists, advocates, and youth leaders — followed by live Q&A and compiled FAQ documents.

  2. 02

    Workshop

    Small-group activities led by speakers on topics tied to real situations in their countries.

  3. 03

    Debate

    Day 2 floor discussion debating key sections of adopted resolutions from regional and cultural perspectives.

  4. 04

    Action

    Selected solutions presented to the full conference and shared on YAC channels.

  5. 05

    Recognition

    Awards for outstanding contributors. All participants receive a certificate of attendance.

05

Who it's for

Young participants

Students and youth advocates aged roughly 14–25 who want to engage with human rights beyond the textbook — through dialogue, debate, and hands-on workshops.

Speakers & hosts

Young professionals, activists, and subject-matter volunteers who can deliver a masterclass or moderate a session. Become a speaker →

Schools & organizations

Educators, youth organizations, and institutional partners looking for a credible, free virtual program their students can join from anywhere.

About YAC

A global summit built by youth, for youth

What is Youth Action Conference?

YAC is a recurring virtual youth summit where participants explore human rights, hear from expert speakers across regions, and engage in workshops, panel debates, and breakout sessions. Every edition runs over two days with four timed sessions so youth from every timezone can join. Participants receive certificates of attendance.

🌍

Voices of the West & East

Sessions are timed for Asia, the Middle East, Africa, the Americas, and the Pacific — so every region has a primary window to participate live.

⚖️

Youth stances on human rights

From freedom of expression to minority rights, speakers address real situations in their countries through masterclasses and workshops.

Who runs it?

Youth Action Conference is organized by IOY Ambassadors — young leaders volunteering their time to create spaces where students can lead. This is an independent initiative and is not officially endorsed by any institution.
